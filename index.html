THE BIG PICTURE

First, understand
the test.

IELTS stands for International English Language Testing System. It tests how you use English across four skills. This guide focuses on Academic preparation.

♬

Listening

~30 minutes

4 parts · 40 questions

Everyday conversations and monologues, academic discussions and lectures.Explore Listening▤

Reading

60 minutes

3 sections · 40 questions

Academic texts totalling approximately 2,150–2,750 words.Explore Reading✎

Writing

60 minutes

2 tasks · 150 + 250 words minimum

Plan about 20 minutes for Task 1 and 40 minutes for Task 2.Explore Writing“

Speaking

11–14 minutes

3 parts · a real conversation

Introduction and interview, a long turn, then a deeper discussion.Explore Speaking

Timing above describes the test sections; Listening answer-transfer arrangements depend on the test format. Speaking may be scheduled separately.
